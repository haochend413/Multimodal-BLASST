How the skip rule works. A tile can be dropped for a row when its best score sits more than gap below the row's maximum, because every entry in it then carries at most e^−gap of the top entry's softmax weight. At gap 1 that is 36.8%; at gap 4, 1.8%. The gap is not free — it follows from BLASST's calibrated threshold as gap = ln(L / threshold_scale_factor), which puts its aggressive settings between 0.6 and 3.4.

Rows are causal, so row i sees only keys 0…i. Captured post-RoPE from scaled_dot_product_attention; weights are the exact softmax, tile maxima the exact scores. Generated by scripts/export_rows.py.
